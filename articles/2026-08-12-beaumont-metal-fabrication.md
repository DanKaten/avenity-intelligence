## Overview

We analyzed 10 metal fabrication and machine shop companies operating in the Beaumont, Texas industrial market to assess their current AI search visibility — specifically, their ability to appear when a plant procurement manager, EPC buyer, or maintenance planner asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews questions like *"who fabricates ASME pressure vessels near Beaumont?"* or *"structural steel fabricator on the Texas Gulf Coast with overhead crane capacity."*

This is a different buying motion than consumer search. Industrial buyers rarely search for a company — they search for a **capability**. Shell-and-tube heat exchangers. Pipe spool fabrication. Skid modules. Precision machining and pump repair. That distinction matters technically, because AI engines answer capability questions by matching structured, separately identifiable entities. A fabricator with eight distinct product lines buried inside one undifferentiated "Services" page presents as one entity to a machine, not eight.

Beaumont and the surrounding Golden Triangle corridor host one of the densest concentrations of code fabrication capacity in the United States. This report identifies where each company currently stands and the technical factors that affect how AI engines read their capabilities.

## Scoring Methodology

Each business is assessed across four technical dimensions using publicly observable signals only:

| Dimension | Max Points | What We Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Entity Clarity | 25 | Clear business description, capability definition, location signals, and whether distinct product lines are separately identifiable |
| Schema & Structure | 25 | Structured data markup, NAP consistency across platforms, technical signals |
| Authority & Reviews | 25 | Publicly detectable credibility signals — certifications, operating history, review and testimonial content |
| Content Depth | 25 | Product-line pages, service pages, FAQ content, project detail, AI-answerable content |

**Score guide:** Strong (85–100) · Good (70–84) · Developing+ (55–69) · Developing (40–54) · Early Stage (below 40)

For this industrial category, Entity Clarity is weighted toward **product-line separation** — whether each fabrication capability has its own addressable page and its own machine-readable identity. That single factor separates the top of this list from the middle.

Scores reflect AI search visibility readiness only. A business with a low AI visibility score may be highly successful, well-regarded, and excellent at its trade — these scores measure one specific technical dimension of digital presence.

- Content Depth (22/25): More than twenty distinct service and capability pages were detected, alongside project and news content. FAQ-format content addressing common procurement questions was not found.

**Highest-impact opportunity:** Adding Service-level structured data to each of the existing capability pages would let AI engines cite STI Group for a specific fabrication scope rather than as a general contractor — the pages already exist, only the machine-readable layer is missing.
