## Overview

We analyzed 10 industrial coatings, abrasive blasting, and tank lining companies operating in the Pasadena, Texas industrial market and the surrounding Houston Ship Channel corridor — Baytown, La Porte, Deer Park, Channelview, Galena Park, and La Marque — to assess their current AI search visibility. Specifically, we looked at their ability to appear when a plant reliability engineer, turnaround planner, or procurement manager asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews questions like *"who applies chemical-resistant tank linings near Pasadena TX?"* or *"SSPC-certified blast and coat shop on the Houston Ship Channel."*

This is a different buying motion than consumer search. Industrial buyers rarely search for a company — they search for a **specification**. Fusion-bonded epoxy. Inorganic zinc primer. Rubber lining for an acid tank. Intumescent fireproofing. AWWA potable water lining. Coke drum insulation. That distinction matters technically, because AI engines answer specification questions by matching structured, separately identifiable entities. A coatings contractor with nine distinct lining and coating systems described inside one undifferentiated "Services" page presents as one entity to a machine, not nine.

The Ship Channel corridor between Pasadena and Baytown carries one of the highest concentrations of refining and petrochemical asset-protection work in North America. This report identifies where each company currently stands and the technical factors that affect how AI engines read their capabilities.

Scores reflect AI search visibility readiness only. A business with a low AI visibility score may be highly successful, well-regarded, and excellent at its trade — these scores measure one specific technical dimension of digital presence.
