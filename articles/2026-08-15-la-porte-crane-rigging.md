## Overview

We analyzed 10 crane, rigging, and heavy haul companies operating in the La Porte, Texas industrial market and the surrounding Houston Ship Channel corridor — Bayport, Barbours Cut, Deer Park, Pasadena, Baytown, Channelview, Texas City, Freeport, and out to Nederland and Corpus Christi — to assess their current AI search visibility. Specifically, we looked at their ability to appear when a turnaround planner, project engineer, construction manager, or logistics coordinator asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews questions like *"who can set a 400-ton reactor vessel near La Porte TX?"*, *"SPMT module transport Bayport terminal,"* or *"tandem lift crane rental for a refinery turnaround Houston Ship Channel."*

Crane and rigging work is not bought as a category. It is bought as a specific lift, a specific move, and a specific set of constraints. The buyer does not search for a crane company — they search for the capability that solves the problem sitting on the schedule. Bare crane rental. Operated and maintained rental. Specialized rigging. Gantry and jack-and-slide systems. SPMT and dual-lane transport. Machinery moving and millwright setting. Lift planning and engineering. Turnaround and shutdown support. Relocation and decommissioning. Transloading. Rapid response.

That distinction is the entire technical story of this market. AI engines answer capability-specific questions by matching structured, separately identifiable entities. A contractor that performs fourteen distinct lifting and transport capabilities, all described inside one undifferentiated "Services" page, presents to a machine as one entity — not fourteen. A contractor that has built fourteen separate pages but attached no capability-level structured data to any of them has done the harder half of the work and skipped the easier half.

This corridor is also unusually geography-sensitive. A crane company's addressable market is bounded by mobilization distance and permit routes, which means the published branch address is not a footnote — it is a primary ranking signal. This report identifies where each company currently stands and the technical factors that affect how AI engines read their capabilities and their coverage.

Scores reflect AI search visibility readiness only. A business with a low AI visibility score may be highly successful, well-regarded, and excellent at its trade — these scores measure one specific technical dimension of digital presence.
